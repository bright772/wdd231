Website Plan

This site will be for my English-tutoring business. It is mostly for parents who may want to decide to enroll their child, so every section below is written with that reader in mind.

Site Name

Bright English Study Training

"Bright" is my English name, so I will use it for this brand. It sounds warm and happy to the parents choosing an English tutor for their child. "Study training" sounds like they will become good studiers in the future.

Site Purpose

The site is a landing page for students' parents. They can find the location, contact information, and details such as class materials or schedule, and the price. I can put pictures of the place or the materials.

Scenarios

Questions a parent visiting the site is likely to have:

  • Where is the classroom located?
  • What is the weekly class schedule?
  • How much do tuition and fees cost per month?

Color Scheme

Four colors:

  • Deep Teal #1F6F78 — header background, headings, nav background
  • Warm Coral #F2664B — links, buttons, and accent highlights
  • Cream #FDF6EC — page background
  • Charcoal #2E2E2E — body text

Typography

Two fonts, both in use throughout this document:

  • Headings (h1, h2, h3, nav, buttons): Baloo 2 — a rounded, friendly display font that fits a children's-education brand.
  • Body text (paragraphs, lists): Nunito Sans — a clean, highly legible sans-serif so parents can comfortably read schedule and pricing details.
